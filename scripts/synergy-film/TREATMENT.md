# Offline

*A 60-second wordless film about Synergy. 24 fps, frames 1-1440, 14 shots, 3 environments, one licensed track. Built procedurally in Blender 4.5 and rendered with Cycles.*

**Logline.** Synergy, the deadpan host of every meeting, praises each problem and posts it through the sip hole of the coffee he never drinks. It lands in Offline, a vast world inside the cup where everything nobody solved slowly turns into nature. When the same padlock comes back for the six-hundredth time, he unplugs the meeting and takes the first sip of his life. The world tilts, because his meeting was inside the cup all along.

## Story

**The story.** We open inside a world with no name: a dark espresso sea under a ribbed white sky, ringed by cliffs of glowing paper hundreds of metres high and lit by a slot-shaped sun. Something huge eclipses the sun. Match cut: the sun was the sip hole of a coffee lid, and ivory robot fingers are posting a sealed envelope through it. The camera pulls back to reveal Synergy, standing at the head of a thirty-metre walnut table. His colleagues are nine beige CRT terminals sitting in office chairs, people shown only as coloured light; Pat's screen is dead. The printer attends from its own chair at the foot, and every terminal is wired by a yellow coiled cord into the conference hub in front of him. The beat drops. Kevin's sky-blue terminal pulses as it 'speaks' and sends a brass padlock gliding the length of the table. Synergy stops it with one finger and studies it. One wave of light crosses his LED mouth ('Outstanding.'), and without looking down he posts it into his cup. Inside, the padlock tumbles out of the sun and lands at the foot of an arch built from six hundred older padlocks, the oldest weathered into lichen and stone: Kevin has been waiting for access for a very long time. The meeting speeds into a deadpan machine. A glass zero, envelopes, spreadsheet tabs and finally a 300-page binder delivered by the paper droid are each stopped, praised and posted on the beat, faster and faster, while the cup never moves a millimetre. Inside the cup, everything nobody solved has become landscape. The zero is a ring atoll, the envelopes refold into a murmuration of origami birds, and the binder's pages pour down a cliff as a paper waterfall. Then the padlock comes back: same colleague, same scratches, same rhythm. For the first time, Synergy looks down at a problem. His praise starts across the mouth row and stalls halfway. He pulls the master jack out of the table, and the music dies with the click. The terminals collapse one by one into horizontal lines, the printer stops mid-sheet, and in the dark, Pat's screen finally comes on. Synergy gives the film's only head dip, then lifts off his headset and sets it down. Inside the cup it is night, and fireflies in the colleagues' colours fall into step over mirror terraces. Alone, Synergy raises the cup for the first time in his life. His mouth row fills like a progress bar, his eyes close to two thin lines, and the room begins to tilt. The camera rises through the slot-shaped skylight: the meeting room is a small box on an island in the middle of Offline, standing in the beam of the sip hole, and the whole world is tilting toward his own sip. White. Black. In the dark, the printer feeds one more sheet.

**Why it is the Synergy story.** In canon, Synergy hosts every LinkedIn Beats meeting, and his ritual is to praise every problem ('Outstanding.') and take it offline. Kevin is 'still waiting for access', Linda carries the zero forward, the welcome email went to spam and Strategy wants a third tab. The printer produces a 300-page framework and never stops, the droid has a very important delivery, and Pat never turns the camera on. 'Same link. Same time. Same people.' He holds a coffee he never drinks. The film plays each of these without a word and makes his catchphrase physically literal: offline is a place, and it is inside the cup. The word itself is syn + ergon, 'working together', and Buckminster Fuller defined synergy as the 'behavior of whole systems unpredicted by the behavior of their parts taken separately'. The meeting performs the buzzword and resolves nothing. Offline, with no one supervising, the abandoned parts organise themselves into a living system: a lichen-crusted arch (lichen is a fungus and an alga living as one organism), a murmuration in which each bird tracks seven neighbours, and fireflies falling into Huygens' 'odd kind of sympathy'. Real synergy happens where nobody is looking. The finale is 'let's circle back' as a strange loop, because the host's own meeting sits inside his own coffee.

**Director's call.** The spine is candidate C, the world inside the cup. Grafts:
- A: the repeated padlock as the visible cause of the turn.
- A: the yellow coiled cord and the macro jack pull, with the music dying on the click.
- A and C: CRTs sitting in office chairs.
- A: the paper waterfall, moved inside the cup.
- D: taking the headset off before the sip.
- B: comic stillness, with the Huygens sync carried only by the fireflies.
Canon fixes: he stands throughout, and the badge is a blank card with a small cobalt square. The eyes are 2x3, as in the user's attached image. The mouth is one fixed-height row, and there is exactly one head dip in the film. Cut: time-lapse morphs (every landform is shown already formed, which reads as time), the folder mesa, the calendar stones, the stakeholder field, the clock cold open, the unseen third party at the sip, and all walking. The result is three environments, fourteen shots and one licensed track.

## Environments

### E1_OFFLINE

The inside of Synergy's lidded paper cup, built as a world at x9,000 scale: about 650 m across at sea level, widening to 810 m at the rim. An espresso sea fills it to 40% of its height. Above the sea, translucent paper cliffs rise about 600 m, leaning gently outward with the cup's taper and split once by the vertical seam fold, up to the rolled rim where they meet the lid. The sky is the lid: a ribbed white dome about 80 m high with concentric embossed rings. Near its edge the rounded-oblong sip slot (about 135 x 45 m) burns as the sun. Its single slanting shaft of light falls to a small stone island near the centre, where the meeting room stands as a dark box. The padlock arch stands under the slot, near the cliff. Over the film the world holds four landforms made from deferred problems: the padlock arch, the ring atoll, the green tab terraces with water mirrors and the paper falls on the far cliff. It also holds an origami murmuration and, at night, synchronized fireflies. There are two lighting states, day (meeting on) and night (meeting unplugged), plus a gravity tilt rig for the finale. Used in S01, S06, S08, S12 and the exterior half of S14. Camera clip 0.1 m to 6,000 m.

**Key assets**

- Cup shell: tapered interior wall with a translucent paper-fibre shader carrying three octaves of fibre noise (1 m, 10 m, 100 m) so it reads at kilometre scale; one vertical seam fold; rolled rim
- Lid dome: concentric embossed ribs in a white polystyrene shader (roughness 0.35); emissive rounded-oblong sip slot about 135 x 45 m near the dome edge
- Slot shaft: emissive gradient cone with alpha falloff and noise breakup (no volumetrics); gold-white by day, moon-silver by night; lands on the central island
- Exterior colour blooms: large area lights outside the cup wall, one per attendee colour, glowing through the paper and switched off one by one in step with S11
- Espresso sea: Ocean modifier at low amplitude with capillary normals; base #2a160b shading to amber in the shallows; crema-foam shoreline (procedural mask) and instanced foam floes
- Padlock arch (about 110 m): about 600 instanced padlocks (8-14 m) along an arch curve. A per-instance age attribute drives brass, then brown patina, then verdigris #43a08a, then lichen-crusted stone with chartreuse #e5ff8d and frost-grey lichen. Scree of padlocks at its foot, instanced small pines on the crest, plus the hero padlock for S06 (keyframed fall, bounce, settle)
- Ring atoll: a 150 m ring island derived from a torus, with ivory sand, about 400 instanced palms and a lagoon with a warm caramel subsurface glow
- Tab terraces: a hill of seven stepped green terraces with flat water-mirror paddies (roughness 0.02) and instanced rice-grass
- Paper falls: a ledge two-thirds up the far cliff. About 20,000 instanced blank A4-proportion sheets (about 2 m at world scale) run on a ballistic spline with curl-noise flutter and per-sheet spin. Alpha mist cards and a heap of sheets at the base. At night it thins to a trickle of about 500 sheets
- Origami murmuration: about 4,000 low-poly white origami birds (about 6 m across). Boids are precomputed in Python (each bird tracks its 7 nearest neighbours) and baked to a point cache, with a wing-flap shape key. One hero envelope unfolds into a bird (S08). Roosting instances sit along the arch crest at night
- Fireflies: about 1,500 emissive points in the nine attendee colours (one grey). Each instance's blink phase converges to unison on a baked Kuramoto-style curve
- Stone island and room box: a 30 x 6 x 5 m dark, flat-roofed box with the rounded-slot skylight glowing, standing in the shaft's pool of light
- Depth haze from a compositor mist pass. A gravity rig (an empty) rotates the sea plane, the beam pool and loose instances 0-12 degrees for S13-S14, while the cup geometry stays fixed

**Look.** Day reads as an enclosed vastness, an 'afternoon inside a cup'. Near-black espresso water carries a gold glitter path. The translucent ivory cliffs glow at 3200K with soft blooms of cobalt #0757ec, acid yellow #fff349 and pink #ffb9dc under a 7500K slot-sun, with warm-grey aerial haze, deep green #17372e and lime #e5ff8d foliage and paper white. Painterly, restrained and never busy: four landforms only. Night: a moon-silver shaft and dark cliffs with one faint grey bloom (Pat), and all colour comes from the fireflies and their reflections.

The hub's own yellow coiled master cord runs 40 cm to a brass socket in the table's head edge. ## Music and sound

**Source rule**. One licensed recording and real recorded SFX only; nothing is synthesized in code. Processing is limited to edits, EQ and low-pass filtering, volume and pitch shifts of recordings.

**Score**. 'Night Sky Hip Hop', Mixkit stock music #970 (audio/raw/music_lofi/mixkit-970-night-sky-hip-hop.mp3), under the Mixkit free licence for video. It is a relaxed, dusty hip-hop groove measured at 89.9 BPM, close to the 92 BPM of the canon stand-up. Its first two bars are a lighter intro with less kick, and the full kit enters at 5.33 s into the file.

**Grid**. Picture is cut at 90 BPM, which at 24 fps is exactly 16 frames per beat and 64 frames per bar. Beat b starts at frame 16b+1, and 90 beats make 1,440 frames. If the beat map drifts, conform the audio by less than 0.2% rather than moving cuts.

## Risks and mitigations

- Scale of Offline: the cup world must read as vast, not as a miniature. Use deep focus, aerial haze, tiny surface detail (capillary ripples, foam floes, multi-octave paper fibre) and slow camera speeds. Never use tilt-shift-style shallow focus in the wides.
- The S01 to S02 match cut only works if the slot sits at the same screen position, size (about 20% of frame width) and orientation in both frames. Lock both cameras to a shared screen-space target before animating anything else.
- Hand contact (posting objects, lifting the padlock and binder, the jack pull, the headset lift) is the most likely place for cheap-looking animation. Use Child Of constraints with keyed influence swaps and keep the cup rigidly locked to hand.R. Hide any interpenetration inside the 6-12 frame shrink through the slot. Build the stop, sweep and post cycle once as an action clip and reuse it in S07.
- LED face canon: the mouth is one fixed-height row of 22 LEDs whose brightness travels left to right. The stalled sweep and the sip's progress bar are still brightness only. The eyes are 2 wide x 3 tall, as in the user's attached image; the brief said 3x3, so flag this to the user and keep 2x3 unless told otherwise. No green may spill onto the chassis (glare only on an LED-only pass).
- Heaviest frames are S08 (4,000 boids birds, about 20,000 falling sheets, atoll palms, motion blur) and the S14 exterior. Bake the boids and sheet paths to caches, instance them with geometry nodes, render the flock and the falls as separate passes, and send these shots to the farm first.
- Fireflies: tiny emissive points cause Cycles noise and get smeared by denoisers. Render them as a separate emission-only pass without denoising and composite them with glare.
- The finale tilt can read as a rigid plane swinging. Add a long travelling swell, a foam line climbing one cliff and gravity cues on loose instances, and keep the camera locked to the cup frame so the tilt is unmistakable.
- Clarity of the strange loop: the slot-shaped skylight must be clearly established (the S03 wide, and its reflection in his face glass in S02 and S05), and the box in the shaft must be planted in S08. In S14, hold the box and its glowing slot at frame centre for at least 20 frames before rising away.
- Render budget: 1,440 frames at 60-90 s each is about 24-36 machine-hours before any re-renders. Use adaptive sampling and denoising: 128-256 spp in the Long Room (emission-lit and cheap) and up to 512 spp for the Offline wides. Check every shot at quarter resolution first.
- Text-free QC: the CRTs show only colour fields, scanlines and light bands; the badge is a blank card with a small cobalt square; paper, binder, envelope seals, padlock and printer carry no letters, numbers or logos. Review every frame for accidental glyphs, including in textures and reflections.
- Music sync: the 16-frame grid assumes 90 BPM. Verify the beat map of Night Sky Hip Hop by ear before locking picture. The jack click (f961) and the final stop (f1409) need clean edit points on a kick.
- Six recordings are not yet on disk: a jack unplug, a paper-cup sip, objects dropped into a paper cup, a caster rattle, a paper-cup creak and a ship-hull creak. Source real recordings with clear CC0 / CC BY or royalty-free terms and log the licences.
- Restraint: four landforms only. No time-lapse morphs, extra props or camera shake. The comedy depends on locked cameras and stillness (S09, S11, S13).
- Canon guardrails: Synergy stands and never walks, and the cup stays at chest height until S13. There is exactly one head dip (S11). The printer body never moves, and its sheets only go outward onto a stack that only grows. The droid's tow bar stays rigid, and Synthia appears only as an ivory terminal.
