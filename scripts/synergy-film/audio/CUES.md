# Offline: cue report

The complete 60-second soundtrack: `audio/cues.json` (163 cues), generated by `audio/build_cues.py`, rendered by `audio/mix.py` to `out/audio/mix.wav` and checked by `audio/verify_mix.py`.

```sh
python3 audio/build_cues.py
python3 audio/mix.py audio/cues.json --out out/audio/mix.wav --stems out/audio/stems
python3 audio/verify_mix.py --json out/audio/mix.verify.json
```

Only recordings from the licensed library and the licensed score are used (66 files, all in the manifest). Processing is editing, gain, filters, tape-style pitch (resampling), panning and convolution with three **recorded** room impulse responses. Nothing is synthesized: no tones, noise or dither.

Each reverb has its own return bus. The returns are muted on the hard cuts so that no tail crosses into another space, and all of them are dead from f1409.

Voxengo's free IR pack was checked and rejected: its page says every impulse "was created with Impulse Modeler", so the files are modelled, not recorded. Also rejected were EchoThief (its licence forbids sync to film or video without a commercial licence), the MIT IR Survey (no licence stated), St. Jakobus Ilmenau (the file metadata says CC BY-NC-SA, which conflicts with its record) and the Farina archive (educational use only). The IRs are extracted unaltered from their Zenodo containers by the `extract` recipes in `tools/fetch_audio.py`.
