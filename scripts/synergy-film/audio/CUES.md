## Recordings the treatment asked to source, and what stands in

The treatment lists six recordings "to source", and the S12 note asks for a distant frog. Freesound refuses this network (HTTP 403); the indexes searched for this library (about 10,000 Sonniss GDC bundle file names, the USC collections on the Internet Archive and the Berklee sample library) list no paper-cup creak, no objects dropped into a paper cup, no 3.5 mm unplug and no laser-printer feed. These are the substitutions, all real recordings:

- **A paper-cup creak (S01):** a small cardboard box squeezed (Sonniss, EffectsWorks), two octaves down. Same fibrous material, larger panel.
- **Objects dropped into a paper cup (S07 posts):** single water drops into wooden and metal vessels (USC Gold Tape, CC0), one isolated drop per post. The library's sugar-into-a-cardboard-cup take is a 2 s granular pour with no single drop or pitch, so it cannot carry the melody; no marble, coin or pebble take falls into a cup as one clean, pitched event.
- **A 3.5 mm jack unplug (f961):** a close-miked 3.5 mm TRS patch-cable plug-in (Bolt, ARP 2600 panel) with a small metal tick. It is an insertion, not a pull: the same metal-on-metal contact click, without the pull's scrape.
- **A sip from a paper cup (f1297):** a close, soft sip of a hot drink (Mixkit); the cup is not named.
- **A ship-hull creak (S13-S14):** real wooden ship creaks (USC R28-03 large wooden ship; USC G27-11 ship creaks).
- **One laser-printer sheet feed (f257, f321, f737, f1049-1060, f1418):** one take of a real desktop printer (Sonniss, Robert Cordova "Printer 02"; the printer type is not stated). Every printer sound in the film is now this one take.
- **A distant frog (S12), new in rev2:** Mixkit #1241 "Forest ambience with croaking frogs" (Mixkit SFX licence), its frog section (45.8-50.2 s). Downloaded from its Mixkit file URL and logged in both `out/audio/raw/manifest.json` and `audio/library.json`. A Sonniss GDC 2017 rice-field take (Charlie Atanasyan) was also checked: it is a dense insect chorus with no single frog, so it was not used.

**Library sounds that cannot be verified as recordings** (kept, noted here): the Mixkit "transition" swooshes #1474 (S05/S06 fall whoosh) and #3161 (S14 flying swoosh) are gated to digital silence, and the Alexander Kopeikin "sci-fi metal" steel bulkhead hit (S06) is probably a designed library sound.
