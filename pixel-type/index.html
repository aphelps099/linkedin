Reel Peg Board

A typeface you plug in.

Every letter is a set of pegs in a black board. Light them on the beat and the words land with the music. Nothing is drawn. Everything is lit.

This is the proof sheet for All Hands: the faces, the light, the timing and the rules every frame obeys. Sign at the bottom.

01

Faces

Three hand-drawn capital faces. Every glyph is a grid of pegs; nothing is traced from an outline font.

02

Scale

Type grows in whole pegs. One glyph bit becomes a k × k block, k from 1 to 4. The board never resamples, so every size is as sharp as the first.

03

Palette

Four tones of light, each a ring, a hot core and a glow. Past full power a peg burns toward white. Type is never green: green is Synergy's face.

04

Kerning

Automatic and collision-proof. A pair closes by one peg only when no lit peg would touch its neighbour, diagonals included, and the average gap stays at 2.5 pegs or more.

    05

    Reveals

    How a word arrives. Each one has a job in the song, and all eight are pure functions of time: preview, scrubbing and export light the same pegs.

    06

    Formats

    Paste a post. The fitter picks the largest pitch that keeps the type inside each format's safe area, breaks at punctuation where it can and never strands a short word.

    07

    The 57

    Every phrase the lady recorded, set by the same fitter. Press one to hear it: the pegs light on her measured word boundaries, to the millisecond.

    08

    Output by department

    The equalizer lives on the board. Four bands per player, each over the range that player's stem actually speaks in, with a white peak peg. On a kick, Ops and Pat slam to full. The lady's group only clocks in for the drops.

    Demo signal at 120 BPM. In the song, every band is the stem itself.
    09

    Psych

    The board as an image. Eight looks lit on the same pegs as the type, in the same four tones. The voices carry the light, the claps swell the pegs, the beat turns every cycle. Words always win.

    Demo drive at 108 BPM: claps on 2 and 4, a voice two bars on and two off. In the film, the song drives it.

      10

      House rules

      What every frame obeys, live or exported. The checks in scripts/ fail the build if one breaks.

      1. One pitch per shot. Type grows in whole pegs and is never resampled.

      2. Every socket shows. An unlit peg is still a peg, kept at 16/255 or brighter so video never crushes it.

      3. Light leads sound. A peg may arrive up to 25 ms early. It is never late.

      4. Three flashes a second. The most allowed on the whole board or on any quarter of it. The governor tones down the fourth before it happens.

      5. One accent word. A hook gets one word in signal yellow. Never two.

      6. Never green. Green is Synergy's face.

      7. Words win. Psych imagery goes dark a peg around lit type, dims near it, and never relights a letter.

      8. Pegs leave their sockets on purpose. Detune, rain and the tape stop. Nothing else moves them.
