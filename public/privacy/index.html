LinkedIn Beats is an independent creative and parody project operated by Aaron Phelps / Look Serious. We are not affiliated with or endorsed by LinkedIn Corporation. This policy covers linkedinbeats.com and its music, learning, sharing, and store features.

We use information to provide the features you choose, remember your work, manage newsletter subscriptions, and keep the site running. We do not sell your personal information or use it for cross-site targeted advertising.

What we handle

What you give us

Newsletter signups include your email address, first and last name when requested, signup source, consent, and signup time. If you email us, we receive your address, message, and attachments.

Our tools process the text, names, phrases, audio, and settings you enter. The current synth stores imported samples and saved projects in your browser; importing a sample does not upload it to our server. Learning progress and shopping-cart choices also use browser storage.

What reaches our servers

Voice generation sends the requested vocal lines to our server. Shared beat and certificate links send the information in their URLs when opened or fetched for previews. If you use a media-upload feature, the uploaded file and its associated details reach our server too.

Our hosting and delivery services may process IP addresses, browser information, requested URLs, timestamps, and error or security logs. Some endpoints use IP addresses temporarily to limit abuse. Please keep confidential workplace information out of inputs and shared links. The board does not need to hear this demo.

Sharing is a public-facing deliverable

A shared beat URL can contain your artist name, selected phrases, and beat settings. A certificate URL can contain the name printed on the certificate. Anyone with the link can view or reproduce that content; platforms may fetch and cache its preview. Encoded URL content is not encrypted or private.

You can optionally connect your LinkedIn account to publish a beat video or certificate image with a caption. LinkedIn asks you to authorize basic profile access and permission to post. We request your account identifier and name, not your email, connections, private messages, or feed. We use that identity to show which account will publish.

Connected does not mean published

Connecting alone does not post anything. You review the media, caption, and audience, then choose Publish. At that point we send the selected file through our server to LinkedIn, wait for processing, and submit the post. Your content and resulting post are handled under LinkedIn’s privacy policy.

We store your access token encrypted on private server storage, with your account identifier, name, and limited upload and publishing receipts. An essential secure, HTTP-only session cookie keeps your browser connected for up to 30 days or until the token expires, whichever comes first. A separate connection-security cookie expires after ten minutes. Tokens are not placed in browser storage or shared URLs.

Disconnect in any sharing preview to remove that connection’s token and associated records from our active storage. You can also revoke our access in LinkedIn’s permitted-services settings. Disconnecting does not delete posts or media already sent to LinkedIn; manage those on LinkedIn. Manual sharing and downloads remain available without connecting an account.

Who helps keep the lights on

Render hosts the application and Cloudflare provides media storage and delivery. These providers process information needed to serve the site and protect their infrastructure.

Our current post-beat voices are generated by software running on our server. Other voice features, if enabled, can send requested text to OpenAI or ElevenLabs. If checkout is enabled, Stripe processes payment information and Printify receives the order and delivery information needed for fulfillment. Our application does not collect your full payment-card number.

External links and embedded third-party services have their own privacy practices. We may also disclose information when required by law, to respond to a valid legal request, or to address fraud, security threats, or harm. Service providers may process information in the United States or other countries where they operate.

Storage, without the storage-unit metaphor

  • Browser data: saved synth projects, learning progress, and cart settings remain until you reset them or clear this site’s browser data. Downloaded exports remain wherever you save them.
  • Newsletter records: retained while you are subscribed, unless you request deletion. Limited consent or suppression records may be retained to honor your preferences or legal obligations.
  • Generated voice cache: cleaned based on size and age; the current cleanup uses a seven-day inactivity threshold. Reuse can refresh a clip’s cache age.
  • LinkedIn connection: connection details and publishing receipts are retained for up to 30 days, until expiry, or until you disconnect. Expired records are cleaned hourly. Direct-publishing media passes through server memory during the upload; we do not save a separate permanent media copy for this flow.
  • Uploaded beat-video cache: configured for seven-day expiry and size-based cleanup. This is temporary storage, not a permanent archive.
  • Support and operational records: retained as needed to handle requests, maintain security, and meet applicable obligations. Hosting logs and backups follow provider retention settings.

We use HTTPS for data in transit and restrict access to server-side records. No internet service can promise absolute security. We do not make that promise in a larger font.

You can leave the meeting

Email us to request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information we hold, or to stop newsletter emails. Use an unsubscribe link when provided. We may need to verify that a request is yours and may retain information required for legal or security purposes.

You can clear this site’s browser data to remove local projects, progress, and preferences; save anything you want to keep first. Clearing browser data does not delete server records or files already downloaded to your computer.

Shared links carry their content in the URL. Removing a server cache does not erase a link someone saved, and opening it can regenerate a preview. Delete posts on the platform where you published them; third-party copies and caches may remain. Contact us about sensitive content so we can assess what removal is possible.

Depending on where you live, additional privacy rights may apply, including access, deletion, correction, portability, or objection to certain processing. Contact us to exercise applicable rights. You may also contact your local privacy regulator.

A few final action items

This service is intended for adults and is not directed to children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us so we can investigate and remove it where appropriate.

We will update this page when our practices change and revise the effective date. Material changes to account connections or data use will be explained before you use the affected feature.
