# Meeting studio

The canonical `/studio/` is a charcoal video-meeting window inside the cobalt racing-team website, with black navigation, a thin racing banner, small mic/name badges, green active-speaker borders, Gallery/Speaker view menu, and a familiar bottom meeting toolbar. The music progress strip is inside the meeting window. `/studio-2/` retains the giant ASCII performance stage.

The room uses the existing Studio 02 score, rendered audio packs, Web Audio scheduler, and playlist. It never asks for a camera or microphone and opens no real conference connection. Playback starts only through a user gesture. Clicking an idle participant starts the meeting; after playback begins, clicking a participant spotlights it. The same keyed video elements stay mounted during layout changes. Grid view and Escape clear the spotlight. The Mix panel includes opt-in automatic spotlight direction, beat energy, confidence, voice mode, vocabulary pairs and chorus recall.

Mic buttons change actual audio gain: Strategic Partnerships = drums; People & Culture = keys plus the lift layer; Workflow Automation = robot vocals; Thought Leader = bass. Muting uses a short gain ramp while the sources keep running, so unmuting is phase-aligned. Mutes survive pause, seeking and song changes. Reset mix restores all four parts. Vocal captions use the measured scheduled voice cues; they disappear when vocals are muted. Camera-off “Connecting to audio…” is the fictional participant's running joke, not actual network status.

Three new silent Runway webcam clips are integrated: a corporate man, a corporate woman, and an original rectangular copier appliance robot. Generation task IDs and prompts are recorded in `webcam-briefs.json`. The original dome robot was blocked by the provider and was replaced with a materially different design. These subtle talking/listening loops are not precisely lip-synced to each song.

Media is 960×540 H.264 at 24 fps, CRF 25, with faststart and no audio stream. Each 10-second source is trimmed into a 9.5-second loop, with the first 0.5 seconds crossfaded over the final 0.5 seconds. First-frame WebP posters and cache-versioned URLs ensure the new participants appear before playback. Sampled start/middle/end frames were inspected and the full encoded files decoded without errors.

Asset requests wait for playback. Posters show before play, when videos fail, or when motion is reduced. Video motion pauses with the song, when the tab is hidden, or when offscreen. Muting a musical part leaves its camera moving, as in a real call. Stop video pauses and hides all three camera feeds behind named avatars; Start video restores them. Pause motion freezes visuals independently of audio. System reduced motion freezes all animated imagery. Leave pauses/cancels playback and resets the song to zero, ready to rejoin. ASCII uses the actual master analyser and caps drawing at roughly 15 fps.

Verification: `node scripts/check-studio2.mjs`, `node scripts/check-meeting.mjs`, and `npm run build`. The meeting check exercises participant gain routing, persistent mute state, silence/reset, director decisions, and server-rendered controls/assets. Media decoded successfully with ffmpeg and posters were visually inspected. Browser control was unavailable, so real-browser visual and audible QA remains outstanding.
