# The Company Store — Art Generation Prompts
Form CS-1 · 5 images per product: 3 model shots, 2 flat "factory" shots. Grid slots are **1:1 square**; the featured row is **4:3**.

## Global style block (prepend to every prompt)
> Deadpan corporate stock photography, mid-2000s office aesthetic played straight. Cool, even, fluorescent lighting, no dramatic shadows. Muted palette: off-white walls, grey carpet, corporate blue (#0a66c2) accents only. Models mid-30s, business-casual, neutral competent expressions — never smiling at camera, never candid-joyful. Composition centered, formal, museum-catalogue framing. Shot on medium format, high detail, slight clinical flatness. No text overlays, no watermarks, no logos except the garment print described.

## Negative prompt (append to every prompt)
> golden hour, lens flare, bokeh, laughing, high-five, startup loft, exposed brick, neon, dramatic rim light, HDR, fisheye, cluttered background
