About the department.

LinkedIn Beats is a family of satirical instruments about professional networking culture that are, regrettably, useful. The department noticed that everyone on the feed is saying the exact same thing, and concluded that the same thing deserved a rhythm section. Everything runs in your browser. Nothing is sent anywhere.

The joke is the exaggeration. The utility is that the output is genuinely good. No login is required, no coaching funnel is attached, and no meeting will be scheduled about your results.
