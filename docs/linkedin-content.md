# LinkedIn content: the first slate

Nine posts, ready to paste. Three a week for three weeks. Every post carries one finished
piece from the Cutting Room, so the feed and the homepage tell the same story in the same order.

## House rules

- **Play it dead straight.** The post never winks. The work does the winking.
- **One joke per post.** Then stop typing.
- **Upload the film itself.** A post with the video in it beats a post with a link to the video.
  Files are listed under each post.
- **One link, last line.** Always `linkedinbeats.com` or a page under it.
- **No hashtags.** Except post 9, where the hashtags are the joke.
- **Reply to every comment in character** for the first hour: "Great question. Let's circle back."
