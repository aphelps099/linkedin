# Cel-adventure edit — version 2

Requested October 29, 2026: revisit the Flying House-inspired hand-drawn cast, add Ken Burns motion, watercolor or marker scene builds, sincere deadpan facial close-ups and a little of Dragon Ball Z's editorial intensity. This is an alternate review cut, not an approval to replace the paper style across the project.

## Direction

Original adult cast identities in vintage ink-and-paint television cel drawing, with flat color shadows and watercolor office backgrounds. Two newly generated still sheets provide four meeting views and two dedicated human close-ups. Prompts and source references: [cel meeting prompts](../../art/cel-meeting-prompts.md). Built-in imagegen was used; no Runway video generation was used.

The reference to action-animation editing becomes shot/reverse-shot, occasional eye inserts, abrupt changes of framing, a held diagonal reaction split, and serious pauses. Performances remain ordinary and sincere. No fight imagery, exaggerated shock expressions, comic symbols or recasting as characters from the reference shows.

Camera movement includes eased push-ins, pull-backs and small lateral reframing. New scene transitions use soft wash masks and broad marker-stroke reveals; text has brief drawn underlines. The existing rounded windows retain equal insets. Long subtitles are split at measured pauses for readability. Existing timed phrase cards remain TT Hoves.

This is animation of still artwork through editing, camera motion and compositor reveals. It is not frame-by-frame character animation or lip sync. The printer page and coffee hand are still poses.

## Unchanged sound

The version-1 MP4's original AAC audio track is copied directly, without re-synthesis, re-mixing or audio re-encoding. Same three cast voices, same 96 BPM score, same words and timing. The image edit has 36 shot sections over the same 90-second duration.
