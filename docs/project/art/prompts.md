# Animated cast study prompts

Built-in image generation; internal concept studies, not approved production assets.

## First study

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: first concept sheet for the LinkedIn Beats animated office ensemble, for internal project art direction, not a website thumbnail. Create a wide, carefully art-directed three-character lineup on warm ivory with subtle grounding shadows. Input 1 is identity reference People & Culture: preserve this adult Black woman's actual facial structure, braided high bun, cream collared blouse, small gold jewelry and black headset. Input 2 is identity reference Operations: preserve this middle-aged man's balding head, actual facial structure, pale blue office shirt, black headset and drumsticks/electronic drum pad. Input 3 is the established Learn Synergy style and identity reference: preserve his cream faceted riveted metal head, green pixel eyes in a black screen, black headset, exposed mechanical joints, cream triangular armor and black lanyard. Include all three, People & Culture at left, Synergy center, Operations at right, comparable chest-up scale, sufficient room for instrument in lower right. Translate the two humans into sophisticated dimensional animation characters in the same tactile material/lighting world as this Learn robot. Adult proportions, restrained shape design and slightly sculpted forms, natural skin color, small eyes and individualized faces; faces must clearly resemble their reference persons. Do not make generic stock avatars or cute oversized heads. People & Culture has patient professionally reassuring neutral expression, hands resting together; Operations has a matter-of-fact expression, holding two sticks over a compact drum pad. Synergy expression stays dry/neutral rather than smiling, holds his ordinary paper coffee cup. Their comedy comes from absolute sincerity. Soft directional studio light, detailed cloth and worn metal, polished editorial composition, slight cinematic depth. No big grins, no comic reactions, no glossy plastic dolls, no whimsical props, no captions, no headings, no microtext, no decorative labels. This is one coherent high-craft cast study, not three unrelated styles. Keep objects physically coherent and complete. Wide landscape.

Review: preserved identifying cues and props, but human rendering remained too photographic for an animated cast. Superseded by the medium study below.

## Sculpted animation study

Use case: style-transfer. Edit this three-person LinkedIn Beats cast study. Preserve exactly the three identities, arrangement, clothes, instruments, colors, hairstyles, headsets, adult ages, posture and neutral serious expressions. Change the rendering medium of the TWO HUMANS decisively: they must now look like expertly handcrafted dimensional stop-motion animation puppets, sculpted painted resin faces with simplified planes, matte tactile surface, carefully designed adult facial features, individually sculpted hair shapes and cloth clothing. NOT photographs of humans, not retouched photos, no pores or photographic skin. Preserve the distinctive facial structure of each reference human; keep the braided high bun and jewelry of People & Culture at left, and the balding head/large eyebrows/pale-blue shirt and drum pad of Operations at right. Preserve Synergy's cream angular riveted head and body, green screen eyes, black headset, lanyard and coffee cup. Translate him subtly to the same crafted miniature-set lighting if needed but do not redesign him. All three must feel like physical models from ONE sophisticated animated film for adults. Slightly stylized planes and proportions but NOT cute, no giant eyes or enormous heads. Low-key deadpan professionalism, no smiles or theatrical reactions. Warm ivory studio background, nuanced soft shadows. Preserve complete hands, drumsticks and drum pad. No typography, no microtext. Wide landscape. The central required change is unmistakably stylized sculpted ANIMATION HUMANS rather than photographic people.
